Privacy

Your privacy, explained simply.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who we are

LikeStays (likestays.co) is an online marketplace that connects guests with hosts who list apartments, villas, guest houses and hotels. Questions about this policy: likestays.co@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Guests: name, phone / WhatsApp number, email (if you give it), and the booking details you choose (property, dates, guests, extras).
  • Hosts: name, email, phone / WhatsApp, city, social or contact links, identity details used to verify you, and your property information, photos and videos.
  • Sign-in: if you use Google or email and password, your sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see your Google password.
  • Usage: an anonymous visitor ID, the pages you open and your country, used for basic statistics.
  • On your device: your language, currency, theme, favourites and a list of your bookings are kept in your browser storage.

How we use it

  • To create and manage bookings and listings.
  • To share your booking details with the other side: the host receives the guest's name and contact details, and the guest receives the host's contact details after booking.
  • To verify and approve hosts and listings, prevent fraud and handle support requests and reports.
  • To understand how the site is used and to improve it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with: the host or guest you are booking with; our team members who review bookings, listings and support requests (including through a private Telegram channel used by our team); and service providers that run the site, namely Google Firebase (database and sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting and delivery), Cloudinary (photo and video storage) and Google Translate (only if you choose another language, in which case page text is sent to Google to be translated). We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Payments

LikeStays does not collect or store your card or bank details. Payment for a stay is arranged directly between the guest and the host.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and listing records for as long as they are needed to run the service, resolve disputes and meet legal duties. You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time (see below).

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing likestays.co@gmail.com. You can clear your browser storage at any time to remove what is saved on your device. If you delete your listing, it is removed from the site.

Children

LikeStays is meant for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
