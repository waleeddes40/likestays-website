About LikeStays

Stay somewhere worth remembering.

LikeStays connects travelers directly with verified hosts — apartments, villas, guest houses and hotels — by the night or by the hour. No middle layers: you see the full price up front and you deal with your host directly.

How it works

01Find your stay

Search by city, dates and guests, or explore on the map and in Highlights.

02Reserve in a minute

Pick nightly, hourly or a special offer, add any extras, and confirm. The total you see is the total you pay — no hidden charges.

03Pay your host directly

Your host's contact details appear as soon as you book. Call, WhatsApp or email them to arrange arrival and payment.

For hosts

List your property for free

Set your own prices, offers and cancellation policy, and get a personal link (yourname.likestays.co) that shows only your stays. LikeStays charges a 10% commission on confirmed bookings.
